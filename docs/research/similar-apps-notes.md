## Sources read in full
- Smoke Free pragmatic RCT, JMIR 2024, n=3,143: app offer 6.8% vs 7.0% six-month abstinence (RR 0.97, not significant); 25.3% uptake; users who took it up 12.7% (non-randomised). https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11607577/
- Meta-analysis, JMIR 2023, 9 RCTs, N=12,967: pooled OR 1.25 (P=.06); apps alone OR 1.03; apps + medication OR 1.79 (3 trials); higher adherence OR 1.48. https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10160935/
- Exist 2025 user survey (Feb 2026): top reason "insights to change my behaviour" 48%, "all my data in one place" 31%; 17% say manual entry is too much effort or easy to forget. https://exist.io/blog/exist-survey-2025/
- "Why people quit YNAB": price $14.99/month, data loss on cancel; promotional post, weak evidence. https://dev.to/shahmeer/why-people-are-quietly-quitting-ynab-and-what-they-are-switching-to-1k9g
- Failed to load: https://dev.to/22gstudios/streak-apps-taught-me-one-missed-day-undoes-six-weeks-the-science-says-it-doesnt-so-i-rebuilt-my

How Sol keeps making you better

Four fields looked at: second brain, AI agent building, learning science, and the sociology of self-improvement. They agree on one thing.

The essence

Sol improves you by closing small loops, not by adding things.

People get sharper through a few plain habits done often: recalling instead of rereading, explaining in their own words, getting feedback, sleeping and moving. No app or brain game does it for you. Sol's job is to make those habits almost free, and to change itself one small step at a time, based on your own data.

That is also the fix for what sank the earlier app. The rule is one in, one out.

Every Saturday

Every learning session

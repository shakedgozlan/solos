Two home screens to try

Try it: open Goals, go back Home, then open Ideas. In A, tap or swipe up on the line at the bottom to go Home, and swipe sideways on it to jump back to the last app. In B, swipe Home sideways, and use the three buttons at the bottom. Grey shapes only; colour and style come much later.

Recommended

A · Like an iPhone

One home screen: a big live widget for the main app, app icons with small numbers, and a dock with record in the middle. Inside an app, a thin line at the bottom takes you Home.

For
  • It works the way your own phone already does, so there's nothing new to learn.
  • Everything is on one screen, and the numbers show what needs you.
  • Record sits in the dock, under your thumb.
Against
  • Gestures are invisible until you know them.
  • When Sol grows past about a dozen apps, it needs a second page, like B.
·

B · Like a Galaxy

Two home pages you swipe between: live widgets first, then all the apps. Inside an app, three buttons at the bottom: recent apps, Home and back.

For
  • The first page shows more live information at once.
  • Visible buttons: you never need to remember a gesture.
Against
  • The buttons take a strip of every screen.
  • Half the apps sit on a second page, one swipe away.
  • It works differently from the iPhone in your hand.

Evidence. People spend most of their time in other apps, so they expect a new one to work like the ones they know (Jakob's law, NN/g; Opinion, widely used). You use an iPhone every day (iCloud, Downtime), which favours A. Gestures are harder to discover than visible buttons (NN/g; Moderate, read through summaries), which favours B for a new user, but Sol has one user who already lives with iPhone gestures. About half of phone use is one-handed, and the bottom middle is easiest to reach (Steven Hoober, 1,333 observations, 2013; Moderate).

A limit to know. Sol runs inside your iPhone. A swipe up from the very bottom edge always goes to the iPhone's own home screen, not Sol's (Assumption: how iOS treats installed web apps; we check it on your phone in 3e). So Sol's Home line sits just above that edge, and a tap on it works too.
